## My Thoughts

Austin Kleon's manifesto on creativity is a refreshing reminder that nothing is truly original — and that's liberating. The book encourages you to embrace influence, remix ideas, and start creating without waiting for permission or perfection.

### Key Takeaways

- Nothing is original — all creative work builds on what came before
- Don't wait until you know who you are to get started
- Write the book you want to read
- Use your hands — step away from the screen
- Side projects and hobbies are important
- Share your work and let others remix it

### Favorite Quotes

<Quote author="Austin Kleon">
  Every artist gets asked the question, "Where do you get your ideas?" The
  honest artist answers, "I steal them."
</Quote>

<Quote author="Austin Kleon">
  You don't want to look like your heroes, you want to see like your heroes.
