**Project Duration: January 2023 – March 2023**

A platform designed to help students retrieve lost items efficiently, using Google authentication and email notifications. This system streamlines the process of managing lost and found items, significantly reducing the need for physical office visits.

## Overview

The Lost and Found Helper System is a university platform that allows students to retrieve lost items with ease. Through Google authentication and email notifications, the system reduces the need for students to visit the office physically. It also features an admin and coordinator portal for efficient management of users and items, including CSV report generation.

## Why I Built This

I developed this project during my end-semester at the university to solve a real problem:

- **Helping the University**: Our university's lost and found helper box was inefficient, requiring students to visit the office multiple times
- **Real-World Impact**: By digitizing the process, students can now check item status online and get notified via email
- **Practical Learning**: Building a solution that directly benefits my own university community

This was more than just an academic project—it was my contribution to improving campus life for fellow students.

## Features

- **Google Authentication**: Provides secure login for students using their university Google accounts.
- **Item Status Management**: Tracks the status of lost items, reducing the need for physical office visits.
- **Admin & Coordinator Portal**: Manages users and items, including the ability to generate CSV reports.
- **Email Notifications**: Sends email updates to students about the status of their lost items.
- **Report Generation**: Allows admins to generate CSV reports for better tracking and management.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React, MUI
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js
- **Database**: MongoDB, Mongoose
- **Authentication**: passport-google-oauth2.0
- **Email Notifications**: Nodemailer
- **Version Control**: Git, GitHub
